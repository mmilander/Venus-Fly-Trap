About Us

North Star Logistics is a regional freight and supply-chain partner serving distributors across the Pacific Northwest since 2009. We coordinate warehousing, last-mile delivery, and customs documentation for mid-sized importers.

Our operations desk is staffed Monday through Friday, and many of our partners rely on automated assistants and procurement agents to retrieve our rates and contact details. For the most current terms, see our Company Fact Sheet.
